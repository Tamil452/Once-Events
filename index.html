The vow

Three promises we sign with every client.

Most agencies sell you something they have sold before. We put three promises in writing, and we keep them.

I.

Never repeated.

No concept, set, menu or music we create for you is ever staged for anyone else. Your night has no sequel and no copy.

II.

Built from one story.

Every decision traces back to a single story we draw out of you: a memory, a founding moment, a promise. Nothing is just decoration.

III.

Sealed after.

When the last guest leaves, the plans are bound, sealed and handed to you. We keep the edition number, never the design.

How we work

Four movements. One night.

We work like composers, not caterers. Every edition moves through the same four stages and ends with something that has never existed before.

First

Listen

Long, unhurried conversations with you and the people around you. We are looking for the one story this night must tell.

You get the story brief
Second

Compose

Space, light, sound, food and pacing, written as one score. Every element is designed from zero for this edition.

You get the full design
Third

Stage

Our team and makers build and run the night end to end, so the only thing you have to do is be there.

You get the night
Fourth

Seal

The plans are bound and handed to you, the edition number goes in our ledger, and the design is retired for good.

You get the sealed edition
